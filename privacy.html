Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version. Your calendar and journals live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Jigsaw has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and never sells your information. If you use Links, the things you choose to share are stored in iCloud so your links can see them, and you can delete your Links account at any time from the app.

This policy explains what Jigsaw Calendar ("Jigsaw", "the app") stores, where it's kept, who can see it and how to delete it. Jigsaw is made by Hugo Ayrton Chan ("I", "me"). Questions? Email jigsawcalsupport@gmail.com.

1. On your iPhone

These are kept on your device and aren't sent to me:

  • Calendar entries, loose plans and their lists
  • Daily journals, including writing, drawings, photos, stickers and your song of the day
  • Your puzzle pieces, puzzle packs and app settings

Jigsaw's widgets read this same information on your device to show it on your Home Screen.

2. Your iCloud backup

Jigsaw copies your calendar, journals, pieces and settings to your own private iCloud storage, so they come back if you reinstall the app on an iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. This backup is stored by Apple in your iCloud account. I can't see it. You can remove it in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Jigsaw.

3. Links (optional)

Links lets you connect with friends. It's optional: you only use it if you choose a username. When you do, the following is stored in Jigsaw's iCloud database (run by Apple) so your links' copies of the app can show it:

  • Your profile: username (which you can change once a month; your old one becomes free for others), bio, profile photo, the puzzle piece you wear, your journal stars, and your card's colour and background. People can find your profile by your username so they can send you a link request.
  • Your day, shown to your links: your status, your song of the day, and whether you journalled today. Your daily collage (the drawings, stickers and photos in your journal) and daily journal are shared only if you turn them on in Settings → Sharing. When they're off, your links just see that you made one, with a lock.
  • Link requests you send and receive.
  • Shared plans: plans you share or are invited to (title, time, place and who's going), replies, and the plan's chat messages, photos, stickers and reactions.
  • Reactions you add to your links' cards.

The app only shows your day to people you're linked with. This information isn't end-to-end encrypted, so please don't share anything through Links you'd want kept secret.

4. Permissions Jigsaw may ask for

Each one is optional, and you can change it any time in iPhone Settings → Apps → Jigsaw.

  • Calendars: shows your existing calendars (such as iCloud or Google) on the jigsaw, and saves changes you make to them. These events stay on your device.
  • Location (while using the app): suggests nearby places when you add a location to an entry. Your location is used for the suggestion only and isn't stored by Jigsaw.
  • Notifications: your daily summary, journal reminder, entry alerts and Links updates.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are added to your journal, profile or chats.
  • Contacts: when you add a friend from Contacts, only the contact you pick is used, to write an invite message on your phone. Your contacts aren't uploaded.

5. Apple services

  • Song of the day: when you search for a song, your search words are sent to Apple's music catalogue to find matches and previews.
  • Maps: place suggestions and directions come from Apple Maps.
  • Purchases: puzzle packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and I never receive your payment details.

Apple's use of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. What Jigsaw doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No analytics or third-party data collection
  • No selling or renting of your information

7. Deleting your information

  • Links: in Jigsaw, go to Settings → Links → Delete Links account. This permanently removes your username, profile, shared days, statuses, link requests, invites, messages and reactions from Links. Things your friends made (a request they sent you, for example) stay theirs, but you no longer appear in their Links.
  • On your iPhone: deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • iCloud backup: iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Jigsaw.

If you need help, email jigsawcalsupport@gmail.com.

8. Children

Jigsaw isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has created a Links account, email me and I'll help remove it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
