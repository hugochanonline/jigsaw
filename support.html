Support

Need help or found a bug? Email jigsawcalsupport@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and what happened, and a screenshot if you can. I usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I link with a friend?

Choose a username in the Links tab, then go to Settings → Links and enter your friend's username, or tap "Add from contacts". Once they accept, you'll see each other's cards in Links.

Who can see my journal?

Your journal and collage are only shared if you turn them on in Settings → Sharing. When they're off, your links just see a locked panel showing you made one. Your song of the day is always shared with your links.

How do I get puzzle packs?

You earn them by journalling consistently, redeeming codes (Settings → Customize, then Redeem next to "Earn packs"), or buying them in the Store. Every pack holds one piece. Tap Preview next to a pack in the Store to see every piece it can hold and the odds for each rarity.

Will I lose my calendar if I get a new iPhone?

No. Jigsaw backs up your calendar, journals, pieces and settings to your private iCloud. Install Jigsaw on an iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account and they come back.

How do I delete my Links account?

In Jigsaw, go to Settings → Links → Delete Links account. Your calendar and journals stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for what's removed.

A purchase didn't arrive

Packs are added as soon as the App Store confirms the purchase. If one is waiting for approval (for example with Ask to Buy), it's added once approved. If something still looks wrong, email me with the date of the purchase.
